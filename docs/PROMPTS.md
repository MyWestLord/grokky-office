# Prompts: make more Grokky art

The art in this repo is rendered with code, so it stays 100% consistent. Use these prompts to generate new characters, scenes or videos in the same style with Grok Imagine, Midjourney, Nano Banana, GPT-image, Flux, Kling, Veo or Sora.

## The character (paste into every prompt)

```
Grokky: a cute glossy white vinyl-toy mascot shaped like a teardrop whose pointed top curls over to one side
like soft-serve, a tiny glowing dot at the curl tip, two solid black vertical pill-shaped eyes (capsule shape,
rounded ends) both tilted about 20 degrees counter-clockwise, right eye slightly higher than the left, small
pink blush under the eyes, NO mouth, NO nose, short white stubby arms with mitten hands, chunky sneakers with
thick white soles. Premium 3D designer-toy render, soft subsurface scattering, studio lighting, neon rim light.
```
**Negative:** mouth, teeth, nose, pupils, round eyes, fingers, realistic, logo, text artifacts.

## The office (scene)

```
Long neon office corridor at night, glass-walled rooms on both sides, each room glowing a different color
with a giant wall screen (twitter feed, code editor, candlestick chart, stock board, content studio, whale tracker),
```
